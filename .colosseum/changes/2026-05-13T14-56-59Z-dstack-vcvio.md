## Description

Fourth module of the VCV-io refactor sequence. Migrated `Specs/Quartz/Attestation/Dstack.lean` by:

1. **Removing the dead `RtmrLog : Type` axiom.** Confirmed via `lean_verify` and a global `RtmrLog` reference scan: no declaration, theorem, or downstream module references this axiom anywhere in the Quartz Lean tree. Pure dead-axiom removal: -1.

2. **Bundling the (verifier, sound, complete) trio into one record axiom.** A new structure `TdxVerifier` packages
   - `verify : TdxQuote → Option (MrEnclave × UserData)`
   - `sound : verify q = some (mr, ud) → was_signed_by_dstack q`
   - `complete : was_signed_by_dstack q → ∃ mr ud, verify q = some (mr, ud)`

   into one record, with a single axiom `tdxVerifier : TdxVerifier`. The previously-axiomatic `verifyTdxQuote` becomes a `noncomputable def` projection; the previously-axiomatic `verifyTdxQuote_sound` and `verifyTdxQuote_complete` become `theorem`s derived via projection. Public API names preserved verbatim: downstream consumers in `Zkdcap.lean`, `Protocol/Handshake.lean`, `Protocol/Confidentiality.lean`, `Protocol/CrossComponent.lean`, `Protocol/Conservation.lean`, and `Protocol/AuctionDeterminism.lean` re-build with no modification.

The remaining four axioms (`TdxQuote`, `MrEnclave`, `UserData`, `was_signed_by_dstack`) are untouched.

A companion module `Specs/Quartz/Attestation/DstackVCVio.lean` was added. It sketches the truthful verification-oracle model of `verifyTdxQuote` using VCV-io's `OracleSpec` + `OracleComp` machinery, exposes `VerifyTdxQuoteSpec : OracleSpec TdxQuote` and `verifyTdxQuoteOC : TdxQuote → OracleComp VerifyTdxQuoteSpec (Option (MrEnclave × UserData))`, and documents both the soundness-negligibility honesty target (truthful soundness against a PPT oracle-querying adversary) and the completeness-with-preconditions honesty target (genuine signing + collateral freshness + non-revocation). Isolated from `Dstack.lean` to keep VCV-io's transitive instance load out of the protocol-layer's `Decidable`-synthesis path (same pattern as Steps 1, 2, 3).

## Critical methodology finding (honesty-lens) — load-bearing, third instance

The bundled `tdxVerifier : TdxVerifier` record axiom contains TWO classical-Prop verification implications that are **honest under a named computational assumption but classically over-strong as stated**:

1. **`tdxVerifier.sound`**: Truthful under the DCAP-soundness assumption — a quote that passes the verifier was, *with negligible probability of forgery*, produced by a genuine TEE. The classical-Prop form drops the negligibility qualifier, making it vacuously stronger than what DCAP actually guarantees. An adversary with sufficient computational power could in principle forge a quote that passes `verify` without holding a genuine PCK; DCAP soundness is *computational*, not absolute.

2. **`tdxVerifier.complete`**: Truthful under the DCAP-completeness assumption — a genuine dstack TEE whose attestation collateral is fresh and whose PCK chain is non-revoked decodes to its measurement and user-data. The classical-Prop form drops the freshness / non-revocation preconditions. A genuine dstack TEE with expired collateral, or with revoked PCK, will fail `verify` even though `was_signed_by_dstack` operationally holds.

Both implications bucket into the **(d) classical-Prop verification implication that hides a probabilistic / preconditional gap** classification. They differ from Step 2/3's impossibility shape:

| Step | Axiom bundle | Pathology shape | Bucket |
|------|--------------|-----------------|--------|
| 2 | `commitHashE` | Pigeonhole — injection from open-cardinality to fixed-width is *mathematically impossible* | (d) — impossible-as-stated |
| 3 | `commitHashBytesE` | Same pigeonhole shape on byte side | (d) — impossible-as-stated |
| 4 | `tdxVerifier.sound` | Classical-Prop drops computational-soundness negligibility qualifier | (d) — classically-over-strong |
| 4 | `tdxVerifier.complete` | Classical-Prop drops collateral-freshness / non-revocation preconditions | (d) — classically-over-strong |

This is the **third parallel surfacing** of the (d)-bucket pattern in the VCV-io refactor. Steps 2 and 3 surfaced two instances of the impossibility shape; Step 4 surfaces two instances of the classically-over-strong shape. Both shapes are honest-axiom-failures (the axiom is provably true under the named computational/cryptographic assumption, but the *as-stated* form drops the qualifier that makes it true).

Step 3's prediction — quote: *"Step 4 (Dstack) and Step 5 (Zkdcap) are likely to surface analogous shapes for `verifyTdxQuote` (verification-oracle deterministic equality) and `verifyGroth16_sound` (knowledge-soundness deterministic implication)"* — is **confirmed** here for the Dstack side. The shape is exactly what Step 3 predicted.

**The truthful VCV-io statements** are sketched in `DstackVCVio.lean`:

```
-- Soundness (truthful):
∀ (𝒜 : PPT-Adversary VerifyTdxQuoteSpec) (n : security_parameter),
Pr[ verify(q) = some (mr, ud) ∧ ¬ was_signed_by_dstack q
  | (q, mr, ud) ← 𝒜.run(n) ]
≤ negligible(n)

-- Completeness (truthful, with preconditions):
∀ (q : TdxQuote),
was_signed_by_dstack q ∧ freshCollateral q ∧ ¬revoked q
→ ∃ mr ud, verify q = some (mr, ud)
```

where `VerifyTdxQuoteSpec : OracleSpec TdxQuote` is the verification-oracle specification and `verifyTdxQuoteOC` is the `OracleComp` query against it.

### Note on plan target

The refactor plan (`.colosseum/refactor-plan-vcvio.md`) aimed at "8 axioms → ~3" via:

1. Bundling the verifier + sound + complete trio into one VCV-io oracle-handler bundle (analogous to the `commitHashE` bundling).
2. Demoting `was_signed_by_dstack` to a witness predicate produced by the oracle handler.
3. Removing dead axioms (`RtmrLog`).

**Item 1 is done** (with the caveat that the bundle remains a classical-Prop record; the VCV-io-resident oracle-handler formulation is in `DstackVCVio.lean` but not load-bearing yet — Steps 6+ scope).

**Item 2 is deferred** for the same reason Steps 2-3 deferred their parallel demotions: downstream consumers ride on deterministic implications (e.g. `was_signed_by_dstack q ∧ mrEnclaveOf q = some _ ∧ userDataOf q = some _`), not on probability bounds. **Item 3 is done** (`RtmrLog` removed).

The 5-axiom realisation here is the **honest reduction**. It:

- preserves the public API (no downstream theorem needed modification),
- bundles three axioms (function + sound + complete) into one record axiom (-2 net),
- removes one dead axiom (-1),
- promotes three identifiers out of the axiom list to `def`/`theorem`,
- explicitly surfaces the classical-Prop-over-strong shape of the bundled record in the module docstring and this change record,
- documents the deferred-but-tracked status of the `was_signed_by_dstack` demotion,
- inherits Steps 1-3's "honest reduction over forced reduction" principle.

Honest reduction beats forced reduction. The plan's "~3" target requires the `was_signed_by_dstack` demotion, which the OracleComp lift of Step 6 will enable.

## Classifier-routed failures encountered

None. The migration went through on the first lake build attempt. Two design decisions surfaced during planning but were resolved without iteration:

1. **Bundling shape**: `Function.Embedding` (the Step 2/3 pattern) is not the right shape for `(verify, sound, complete)` because soundness/completeness are *bidirectional implications* over `was_signed_by_dstack`, not an injection. Used a `structure TdxVerifier` instead — packages the function and the two proofs into a single record. Same axiom-reduction effect (three axioms → one), same public-API preservation, different shape. Documented in the module docstring.

2. **`RtmrLog` removal**: verified by `lean_verify` reference scan and global `grep` that `RtmrLog` is not referenced anywhere in the Quartz tree outside its own declaration. Pure dead-axiom removal. First dead-axiom removal of the refactor; flagged in the methodology takeaway for documentation.

Neither issue required spec relaxation or code revision.
