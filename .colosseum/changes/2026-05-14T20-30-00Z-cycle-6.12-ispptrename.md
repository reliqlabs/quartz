## What Round A attack #5 was about

The project-standard `IsPPT` predicate in `ProtocolVCVio.lean:288` is
currently a `True`-placeholder:

```lean
def IsPPT {Adv : Type} (_ : Adv) : Prop := True
```

Consequently, any `SecurityGame.secureAgainst IsPPT` statement
quantifies over **all** adversaries — bounded and unbounded alike.
The `*Game_secure_of_*_bundle_secure` packagings phrased their
conclusions in terms of `secureAgainst IsPPT`, but no reader at
the call site could see that the `IsPPT` filter was vacuous. The
risk was that the SecurityGame statements would be read as the
"PPT statement" they are *intended* to become, when in fact they
are strictly stronger (and not the cryptographic statement that
matches the underlying assumptions about Groth16, ECDSA, etc.).

## Why Option (b) instead of Option (a)

Option (a) was: replace `IsPPT := True` with `IsPPT := PolyQueries`.
That is the right end state but requires:

1. **Cycle 6.13** — refactor adversary types to be
   `OracleComp ProtocolSpec`-based, not `ProbComp`-based, because
   `PolyQueries` is defined over `OracleComp (spec n)` (see
   VCVio at `OracleComp/QueryTracking/QueryBound.lean:603`).
2. **Cycle 6.14** — actually instantiate `IsPPT := PolyQueries`
   with the carrier model wired through.

Both are multi-day refactors with build-breaking churn across all
adversary definitions in the protocol layer. Option (b) is
cheap, additive, surfaces the placeholder gap immediately, and
forward-references the real cycles that close it.

## What this closes vs. doesn't

**Closes**:
- Round A attack #5's surface-level concern: any reader at a call
  site now sees `_AGAINST_UNBOUNDED_ADVERSARIES` in the theorem
  name and immediately understands that the `IsPPT` filter is a
  placeholder, not a real PPT bound.

**Doesn't close**:
- The underlying placeholder is still `True`. The actual
  cryptographic content (the `_negl` lifts in cycles 6.4–6.11)
  is unchanged — those carry the real reductions and the real
  parametric negligibility hypotheses, so the security content
  of the lifts is intact.
- Cycle 6.13 (OracleComp ProtocolSpec adversaries) and cycle 6.14
  (`IsPPT := PolyQueries` instantiation) remain queued.
