## Methodology meta-finding for colosseum v0.2

The cycle-6.4-through-6.11 sequence is direct evidence for a methodology lesson: **over-bundling is the default failure mode of mechanically-applied lift sequences**. The original Step 6.0–6.3 plan classified lifts by the union-bound shape implied by an axiom count, not by the actual probabilistic-failure modes in the classical proof. This produced systematic over-bundling: 7 of 8 lifts were inflated.

The Round A "free-symbol tautology" finding was the proximate cause (free advantages let any union-bound shape typecheck), but the deeper cause was the methodology's bundle-count classifier choosing the wrong target. A correct classifier asks: "what classical lemmas does the proof actually consume, and which of those have probabilistic-failure events vs. unconditional ones?"

For the colosseum methodology v0.2 PR currently with the agent: this is worth flagging as ask 6 — **bundle-count derivation must come from per-conjunct failure-mode analysis of the classical proof, not from a static axiom-count classifier**.
