## Description

Fifth and final module of the VCV-io refactor sequence (Steps 1–5 cumulative axiom-reduction phase). Migrated `Specs/Quartz/Attestation/Zkdcap.lean` by:

1. **Bundling the (`verifyGroth16`, `inputs_to_quote`, `verifyGroth16_sound`, `zkdcapVKey`) quartet into one record axiom.** A new structure `Groth16Verifier` packages
   - `vkey : VKey` (the canonical verification key)
   - `verify : VKey → Groth16Proof → PublicInputs → Bool`
   - `inputsToQuote : PublicInputs → TdxQuote` (circuit-level input binding)
   - `sound : verify vkey proof inputs = true → was_signed_by_dstack (inputsToQuote inputs)`

   into one record, with a single axiom `groth16Verifier : Groth16Verifier`. The four previously-axiomatic identifiers (`verifyGroth16`, `inputs_to_quote`, `verifyGroth16_sound`, `zkdcapVKey`) become `noncomputable def` / `theorem` projections. Public API names preserved verbatim: downstream consumers in `Protocol/Handshake.lean`, `Protocol/Confidentiality.lean`, `Protocol/CrossComponent.lean`, `Protocol/Conservation.lean`, and `Protocol/AuctionDeterminism.lean` re-build with no modification.

   This bundling shape **extends** Step 4's `TdxVerifier` pattern: Step 4 bundled (verify, sound, complete) into one record + axiom; Step 5 bundles (vkey, verify, inputsToQuote, sound) — folding in the canonical-vkey constant as well as the input-to-quote binding map alongside the function + soundness. Folding `zkdcapVKey` into the record is the **net-extra** reduction over Step 4's pattern: there is no constructive way to produce a `VKey` inhabitant without another axiom (since `VKey` is fully abstract), so demoting `zkdcapVKey` to a `def` is not possible at this layer; folding it into the bundled record axiom keeps the trust surface to a single named record.

   The remaining three axioms (`Groth16Proof`, `PublicInputs`, `VKey`) are untouched.

A companion module `Specs/Quartz/Attestation/ZkdcapVCVio.lean` was added. It sketches the truthful verification-oracle model of `verifyGroth16` using VCV-io's `OracleSpec` + `OracleComp` machinery, exposes `VerifyGroth16Spec : OracleSpec VerifyGroth16Query` (where `VerifyGroth16Query := VKey × Groth16Proof × PublicInputs`) and `verifyGroth16OC : VKey → Groth16Proof → PublicInputs → OracleComp VerifyGroth16Spec Bool`, and documents the **doubled-negligibility** soundness honesty target (Groth16 knowledge-soundness + circuit-vs-reference-DCAP-verifier equivalence — both computational, summing into one negligibility budget). Isolated from `Zkdcap.lean` to keep VCV-io's transitive instance load out of the protocol-layer's `Decidable`-synthesis path (same pattern as Steps 1, 2, 3, 4).

Discharging the carriers requires a constructive byte-level model of gnark's wire format (proof serialisation, public-input encoding, verification-key serialisation), which is out of scope for this step. Notes on the (d) classification: the `sound` field is bundled into the `groth16Verifier` record axiom, but its LOGICAL SHAPE remains a classical-Prop implication. Bundling does NOT demote it to the truthful negligibility shape. This is the **same pattern** as Steps 2/3 (`commitHashE`/`commitHashBytesE` bundling) and Step 4 (`tdxVerifier.sound`/`.complete` bundling): surfacing the load-bearing trust assumption without yet demoting it. See the honesty-lens finding below.

## Critical methodology finding (honesty-lens) — load-bearing, fourth instance (doubled-negligibility sub-variant)

The bundled `groth16Verifier : Groth16Verifier` record axiom contains ONE classical-Prop verification implication that is **honest under TWO composed named computational assumptions but classically over-strong as stated**:

**`groth16Verifier.sound`**: Truthful under the COMPOSITION of (a) BN254 Groth16 knowledge-soundness (`negligible_groth16`) and (b) zkdcap-circuit-vs-reference-DCAP-verifier equivalence (`negligible_circuit`). A Groth16 proof that verifies under the canonical vkey was — *with negligible probability of forgery, summed over both assumptions* — produced by a prover who knew a witness for the zkdcap R1CS circuit, AND the R1CS circuit faithfully captures DCAP-quote verification semantics. The classical-Prop form drops *both* qualifiers (the cryptographic-soundness one AND the circuit-equivalence one), making it vacuously stronger than what the gnark/zkdcap stack actually guarantees.

This bucks into the **(d) classical-Prop verification implication that hides a probabilistic gap** classification. Specifically, it is a **doubled-negligibility variant of Step 4's sub-shape**:

| Step | Axiom bundle | Pathology shape | Bucket sub-variant |
|------|--------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| 2 | `commitHashE` | Pigeonhole — injection from open-cardinality to fixed-width is *mathematically impossible* | (d) — impossible-as-stated |
| 3 | `commitHashBytesE` | Same pigeonhole shape on byte side | (d) — impossible-as-stated |
| 4 | `tdxVerifier.sound` | Classical-Prop drops computational-soundness negligibility qualifier (PCK-sig unforgeability) | (d) — classically-over-strong, single-negligibility |
| 4 | `tdxVerifier.complete` | Classical-Prop drops collateral-freshness / non-revocation preconditions | (d) — classically-over-strong, preconditional |
| 5 | `groth16Verifier.sound` | Classical-Prop drops *BOTH* Groth16 knowledge-soundness AND circuit-equivalence qualifiers | (d) — classically-over-strong, **doubled-negligibility** |

This is the **fourth parallel surfacing** of the (d)-bucket pattern in the VCV-io refactor. The (d) bucket now has three established sub-variants:

1. **Impossible-as-stated** (Steps 2–3): the axiom violates pigeonhole; provable false in principle.
2. **Classically over-strong, single-negligibility** (Step 4, `sound`): the axiom hides one computational-bound qualifier.
3. **Classically over-strong, preconditional** (Step 4, `complete`): the axiom hides preconditions on external state.
4. **Classically over-strong, doubled-negligibility** (Step 5, new): the axiom hides TWO independent computational-bound qualifiers that compose by union bound.

Step 4's prediction — quote: *"Step 5 is likely to surface a fourth instance of the (d)-bucket pattern around `verifyGroth16_sound` — same classical-Prop-over-strong shape as `tdxVerifier.sound`"* — is **confirmed** here, with the additional refinement that the over-strength is *doubled* (two computational qualifiers in one implication), not single. The expected shape held; the sub-variant is new.

**The truthful VCV-io statement** is sketched in `ZkdcapVCVio.lean`:

```
-- Soundness (truthful, doubled-negligibility):
∀ (𝒜 : PPT-Adversary VerifyGroth16Spec) (n : security_parameter),
Pr[ verify(vkey, proof, inputs) = true ∧
    ¬ was_signed_by_dstack (inputs_to_quote inputs)
  | (proof, inputs) ← 𝒜.run(n) ]
≤ negligible_groth16(n) + negligible_circuit(n)
```

where:
- `negligible_groth16` is BN254 knowledge-soundness (cryptographic; ArkLib roadmap).
- `negligible_circuit` is circuit-vs-reference-DCAP equivalence (software-verification; separate effort).

This decomposition is structurally important for Step 6: the `groth16` summand in the protocol-layer's union bound itself decomposes into two independent negligibility terms.

### Note on plan target

The refactor plan (`.colosseum/refactor-plan-vcvio.md`) aimed at "7 axioms → ~3" via:

1. Modelling `verifyGroth16` as a verification oracle (analogous to Step 4's `verifyTdxQuote` treatment).
2. Bundling `verifyGroth16_sound` and related claims.
3. Demoting named constants (`zkdcapVKey`) to `def`s where possible.

**Item 1 is done at the companion-module layer** (`ZkdcapVCVio.lean`); the core module retains the classical-Prop record bundling (the VCV-io-resident oracle-handler formulation is documentary, not load-bearing yet — Step 6 scope).

**Item 2 is done** with extension (`verifyGroth16_sound` bundled together with `verifyGroth16`, `inputs_to_quote`, AND `zkdcapVKey` into one record axiom).

**Item 3 is partially done — modified scope**: `zkdcapVKey` cannot be demoted to a `def` because `VKey` is a fully abstract type axiom with no constructive inhabitant. The honest move is to fold `zkdcapVKey` into the bundled record axiom. (If `VKey` is ever refined to a concrete byte representation in a future step, `zkdcapVKey` could be demoted to a `def` with a concrete hex value at that point.)

The 4-axiom realisation here is the **honest reduction**. It:

- preserves the public API (no downstream theorem needed modification),
- bundles four axioms (function + canonical-vkey + input-binding + sound) into one record axiom (-3 net),
- promotes four identifiers out of the axiom list to `def`/`theorem`,
- explicitly surfaces the classical-Prop-over-strong (doubled-negligibility) shape of the bundled record in the module docstring and this change record,
- documents the `zkdcapVKey`-cannot-be-`def` finding and its dependence on `VKey` carrier refinement,
- inherits Steps 1–4's "honest reduction over forced reduction" principle.

Honest reduction beats forced reduction. The plan's "~3" target requires either demoting `zkdcapVKey` to a `def` (blocked by `VKey` abstractness) or, in Step 6, demoting `groth16Verifier.sound` to the truthful negligibility-bound formulation (which requires the OracleComp lift).

## Classifier-routed failures encountered

None. The migration went through on the first lake build attempt. Two design decisions surfaced during planning:

1. **`zkdcapVKey` folding decision**: the plan suggested demoting named constants to `def`s per Step 3's pattern (which demoted `rawDomainSep`, `rawBoundContract`, `rawPlaceholderPubKey`). However, those demoted axioms had carrier types with potential concrete witnesses (byte-list / opaque-string structures). `zkdcapVKey : VKey` cannot be demoted to a `def` because `VKey` is a fully abstract type with no constructive inhabitant in Lean. **Resolution**: fold `zkdcapVKey` into the bundled `Groth16Verifier` record axiom as a `vkey : VKey` field. This achieves the same net reduction (one less axiom) without requiring a separate `[Inhabited VKey]` axiom, and keeps the trust surface to a single named record. Documented in the module docstring + this change record.

2. **`VerifyGroth16Spec` index-type choice**: Step 4's `VerifyTdxQuoteSpec` keyed on a single `TdxQuote` (DCAP verification has the PCK chain implicit in the verifier's static state). Groth16 verification is parameterised on the vkey, the proof, and the public inputs; all three are *inputs* to the verifier function. **Resolution**: index by the full triple `VerifyGroth16Query := VKey × Groth16Proof × PublicInputs`. This is structurally honest — two calls with different vkeys can return different answers, so the index must include the vkey. Documented in the companion-module docstring.

Neither issue required spec relaxation or core-module revision.
